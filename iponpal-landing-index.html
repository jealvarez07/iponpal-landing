Questions

Before you even ask.

What's deducted from my salary?

Usually four things: SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contributions (set by your salary bracket), plus withholding tax on what's left. IponPal's take-home calculator shows each one.

Is it really free?

Yes — 100% free while we're in early access, and the take-home calculator needs no sign-up at all. If there's ever a premium tier, a free version stays.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. Logging is manual — about 10 seconds. No bank login, no passwords requested.

Does it work offline?

Yes. IponPal is a Progressive Web App — once opened it runs without a connection, and your data stays on your device unless you turn on backup.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Private, never sold, and you can export or delete everything anytime. No lending, and we never hold your money.
