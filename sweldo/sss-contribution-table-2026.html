SSS Contribution Table 2026

How much of your pay goes to SSS — and how much your employer pays on top. In plain English, for every salary bracket.

Effective January 2026

Every month a slice of your salary goes to the Social Security System (SSS) before you ever see it. In 2026 the total is 15% of your Monthly Salary Credit (MSC). You pay 5%. Your employer pays 10%, plus a small Employees' Compensation (EC) amount. This page shows the full table and lets you check your own pay.

₱

Rounded to the nearest ₱500 salary credit, the same way payroll does it. Floor ₱5,000, ceiling ₱35,000.

What the numbers mean

SSS does not charge a flat percentage of your exact salary. It first maps your pay to a Monthly Salary Credit (MSC) — a rounded figure in ₱500 steps. Every contribution is then a percentage of that MSC, not of your real gross.

The MSC has a floor of ₱5,000 and a ceiling of ₱35,000. Earn below the floor and you still pay as if you earned ₱5,000. Earn above the ceiling and your contribution stops rising — everyone at ₱35,000 or more pays the same.

A note on WISP

If your MSC is above ₱20,000, part of your contribution is routed into the Workers' Investment and Savings Program (WISP) — a mandatory savings fund that earns returns for you on top of your regular SSS benefits. This does not raise your deduction. You still pay 5% of your MSC; WISP only changes where the upper slice of that money is kept.

Questions people ask

How much SSS comes out of a ₱25,000 salary?

Your MSC is ₱25,000, so your employee deduction is 5% of that — ₱1,250 a month. Your employer separately pays ₱2,530 (₱2,500 plus ₱30 EC).

What is the maximum SSS deduction in 2026?

The MSC caps at ₱35,000, so the most you pay as an employee is ₱1,750 a month. Earning more than ₱35,000 does not increase it.

Do minimum wage earners pay SSS?

Yes. If your pay is below the ₱5,000 floor, your contribution is based on ₱5,000, so you pay ₱250. Note that a statutory minimum wage earner is exempt from income tax, but SSS still applies.

Why does my payslip differ by a few pesos?

Some payroll systems round differently at the bracket edges, and your MSC can shift if you have allowances or a different pay period. Your payslip is always the authority. Use this table as the guide, not the final word.
